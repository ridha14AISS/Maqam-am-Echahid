Maqa'am Echahid

The Martyrs' Memorial of Algeria.

Explore the Monument

An iconic concrete monument commemorating the Algerian War of Independence, standing as a proud symbol of the nation's resilience.

Dominating the Algiers skyline, its three soaring palm leaves shelter the "Eternal Flame" and honor the heroes who fought for freedom.

ABOUT THE MONUMENT

Erected in 1982 on the 20th anniversary of Algeria's independence, the Martyrs' Memorial is a breathtaking structure overlooking the Bay of Algiers.

Designed to represent three standing palm leaves, the monument houses the "Eternal Flame" and features statues of soldiers representing different stages of the liberation struggle.
